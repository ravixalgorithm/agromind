# AgroMind v4 — weakness register (viva prep)

Final version = **AgroMind v4** (YieldRank: LightGBM LambdaMART over yield-grounded features +
split-conformal crop sets) on the DES district × season × year data. Every number below is in
`docs/research/results.json` (`v4.numbers`, `v3.*`) or `backend/data/large/stats.json`; protocol
names: **R** random split, **LSO** leave-states-out, **LYO** leave-years-out.

Status: **CLOSED** (fixed, evidence attached) · **MITIGATED** (reduced, residual stated) ·
**DISCLOSED** (a real limitation, stated in the paper) · **OPEN** (needs an action not yet taken).

| 1 | Persistence baseline is close | “Your model barely beats ‘grow what you grew last year’. Why not ship that?” | MITIGATED | LSO hit@1 55.6 % vs persistence 54.5 % (p = 3.2×10⁻⁶, 43,443 paired queries); R 59.7 vs 55.3; LYO 57.8 vs 55.2. The gap is on cold-start queries (no history): LSO 47.2 vs 42.6. On warm queries persistence is ahead under LSO (62.4 vs 61.0). Framing: v4 ≥ persistence overall and is the only option without history. In progress (CC5): a learned persistence blend for warm queries. `v4.numbers: OursHit*, PersHit*, PersHitCold/WarmLSO` | CC5 |
